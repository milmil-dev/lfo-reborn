Design Notes

  • Palette: Warm amber/gold on deep warm-black — firelit shrine mood, NOT the AI cyan-on-dark cliché
  • Typography: Noto Serif TC (display) + Noto Sans TC (body) — high-quality Chinese font pairing
  • Mobile-first: Single-column stacked layout, horizontal scroll for characters, touch-friendly targets
  • Category grid: 1-col mobile → 2-col tablet → 3-col desktop — no cards-in-cards
  • Character avatars: Placeholder initials — will be replaced with actual character sprites/art
  • Animations: Staggered hero reveal, subtle particle drift, pulse scroll indicator — respects reduced-motion
  • No glass/blur cards, no gradient text, no bouncing — all anti-patterns avoided
